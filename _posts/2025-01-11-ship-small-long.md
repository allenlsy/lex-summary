## Begin with a question

A small release is not a rushed release. Define the user problem and the smallest test that can reveal whether your idea helps. Keep the scope narrow enough that the team can understand every moving part.

## Learn in public

Put the slice in front of real users. Their questions and workarounds are valuable signals. Record what you learn, then improve the product in measured steps. This rhythm reduces risk while keeping momentum: each release is both useful software and a better question.
